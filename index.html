PulseLab v2.5 Quick Editing Guide

1. Drag & Move Clips Freely

Touch or click the middle of any clip and drag left or right across the timeline to reposition it. Use the Snap toggle to snap clips cleanly to beat divisions.

2. Direct Edge Trimming, Split & Fades

Click any clip to reveal its glowing Left & Right Trim Handles—drag either edge handle to shorten or extend the clip. Click Split (S) to cut at the playhead, or adjust Fade In / Fade Out sliders.

3. Import Downloaded Music & Beats

Tap Import Audio to browse your device's Downloads, Music folder, or Files for MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, or FLAC tracks.
