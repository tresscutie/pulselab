PulseLab v4.0 Studio Guide

1. Vocal & Instrumental Stem Isolation

Each track has a 3-mode Stem Switcher: Mix (full stereo), Inst (removes center vocals/lyrics while preserving low-end bass below 160Hz), and Vox (isolates center lyrics & vocals). Tap Split Stems to duplicate an imported song into separate Beat and Vocal tracks.

2. Clean Overdub Recording (Zero Bleed)

Hardware Acoustic Echo Cancellation & Noise Suppression filter out backing track playback from entering your microphone. Adjust the Rec Monitor slider to balance backing music volume while singing, and use earphones for studio-grade isolation.

3. 60fps Clip Dragging, Trimming & Timeline Split

Slide any audio clip along the timeline with GPU-accelerated touch dragging, trim edges with the 20px side handles, or tap Split (S) to cut a clip at the playhead.
