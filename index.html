Use Wired Earphones or Headphones

Wearing headphones prevents speaker bleed so your vocal takes stay clean while overdubbing over existing tracks.

Quick Workflow

  1. Arm a track by toggling its R button.
  2. Click Rec on the top transport bar to record after a 4-beat count-in.
  3. Click Save Project to store your session in your My Projects library.
  4. Click Export .WAV to download a stereo mixdown of all active tracks.
